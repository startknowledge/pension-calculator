Privacy Policy

How Army Pension Calculator handles information, cookies, advertising and third-party services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Army Pension Calculator, a StartKnowledge project, handles information when visitors use this website.

Our website is primarily an informational and calculator-based service. We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information and provide users with a transparent understanding of how the website may operate.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, you should discontinue use of the website.

2. Information Entered Into the Calculator

The calculator may ask users to enter values such as date of birth, date of enrolment, retirement information, rank, service period, basic pay, allowances and other calculation-related values.

These values are used to perform calculations and display an estimated result.

Important: Do not enter passwords, bank account credentials, payment-card details, Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, service passwords, OTPs or other highly sensitive information into a calculator field unless a page explicitly and legitimately requires such information.

Where calculations are performed directly in the browser, the entered values may remain within the browser session and are not necessarily sent to our servers. The exact behavior can depend on the technical implementation of the particular feature.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, technical information may be processed by the hosting platform, CDN, analytics systems, advertising providers or security services.

Depending on the services enabled, this may include information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, approximate location derived from technical information, pages visited, timestamps and diagnostic information.

This information may be used for security, performance monitoring, analytics, abuse prevention, troubleshooting and website improvement.

The website or third-party services operating on the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar technologies.

For additional information, please see our Cookies Policy.
