Accuracy & Information Notice

The Army Pension Calculator is an informational tool intended to provide estimates based on the information entered by the user and the calculation methodology implemented by the website.

Actual pension, benefits, allowances, deductions, qualifying service, commutation, revisions, or other applicable amounts may differ. Official government records, pension authorities, applicable rules, orders, and competent authorities should be treated as authoritative.

Website updates do not automatically mean that government pension rules or official policies have changed. Users should verify important information through appropriate official sources.
