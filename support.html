Support

Need help with the Army Pension Calculator? Report a technical issue, calculation concern or website problem.

How Can We Help?

Support is primarily intended for technical problems involving the website, calculator interface, broken links, display issues and similar website functionality.

Support Email
support@startknowledge.in

Before Contacting Support

  • Refresh the calculator page.
  • Check that all required fields contain valid values.
  • Try an updated browser.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Review the Documentation.
  • Check the FAQ for common questions.

Report a Calculator Problem

If you believe the calculator is producing an unexpected result, please describe the issue clearly.

Where possible, include:

  • The name of the calculator or page.
  • A general description of the issue.
  • The browser and device type.
  • The approximate time when the issue occurred.
  • The steps that caused the problem.

Privacy reminder: Do not send passwords, payment-card information, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

Calculation Accuracy Questions

We can consider reports about possible calculation or formula issues. However, support cannot replace an official pension authority or determine an individual's legally payable pension.

For official pension amounts, users should rely on applicable government rules, official records, PPO documents and the relevant pension authority.

Broken Links or Page Errors

If you find a broken link, missing page, layout problem or other website error, please send the page name and a short description to:

support@startknowledge.in

General Contact

Important Limitation

Support responses are not official government pension advice, legal advice, financial advice or a substitute for verification through the appropriate authority.
